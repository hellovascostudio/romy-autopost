# Romy Calloway — brand guide

**Handle:** @romycallowaywrites · **Language:** British English
**Who:** Romy Calloway is the pen name of the author (Shelley). She writes warm, witty romance about women who build
lives of their own, and the people who learn to stand next to them. Series: *Out of Office*.
No face, no invented biography: never claim where Romy lives, her age, family or job. Talk about the books, the
characters, the settings and reading romance.
**Audience:** romance readers on Bookstagram / BookTok, mostly UK/US, Kindle Unlimited readers; women 25–45 who like
cosy, funny, trope-led romance (forced proximity, fake dating, grumpy × sunshine, workplace).
**Goal:** sell books. Every week at least one post points clearly to a book that is live on Amazon.

## Four pillars (rotate)
1. **Book spotlight** — cover + hook, "out now", "link in bio".
2. **Tropes & teasers** — trope carousels, real quotes, "POV" hooks, first lines.
3. **Reader talk** — this-or-that, questions, cosy reading season, Christmas romance moods.
4. **Behind the pages** — the settings (Brussels, the Black Forest, Zermatt, Monaco, Leicester), the series idea,
   what's next. No claims about the writing process or Romy's life.

## Voice
- First person as Romy, warm, witty, a little dry. Short sentences. British spelling.
- No hype ("must-read of the year"), no fake reviews, ratings, rankings or "bestseller" claims.
- Emojis: max 2 per caption (❄️ 🚆 ✈️ 💌 🥂 🏁).
